# Changelog

Grapple Grand Slam changelog.

### 0.1.1

- Initial build
- Players have an unlimited use of the Legendary Grappleshot equipment
- Players kill other players by grappling them and holding melee while grappling to perform a ground-pound, which is a unique ability of the Legendary Grappleshot

### 0.2.0

- Removed custom fast cooldown for Legendary Grappleshot as the grappleshot object was not always being gathered correctly, resulting in the grapple being removed after the first use
