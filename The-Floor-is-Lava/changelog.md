# Changelog

The Floor is Lava changelog.

### 0.1.1

- Initial build
- Not being airborne damages players

### 0.2.1

- Mode settings and metadata updated

- Changed the equipment from Repulsor → Grappleshot

- Changed starting weapons from Ravager Rebound & Mangler → Diminisher of Hope

- Starting weapon Diminisher of Hope → Needler + MA40 Longshot

### 0.5.1

- Made staying within the healing radius of a Repair Field not deal ground contact damage to the player being healed

### 0.5.2

- Changed starting weapon from Needler + MA40 Longshot → Mk50 Sidekick + Impact Commando
